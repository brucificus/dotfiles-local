Northwest Partners (talent mobilization / IT consulting firm, Miami FL, LinkedIn f_C=40660657, domain northwestpartners.com — the nwpartners.com in a JD is the same firm) runs NO public-facing careers board or ATS.

- northwestpartners.com/careers/ is a marketing page. Only CTA is `/become-a-candidate/`, a lead-capture form (no Bullhorn/JobDiva/Ceipal/Greenhouse/Lever embed, no iframe, no job widget).
- Their live reqs are visible only on the LinkedIn company jobs tab: linkedin.com/company/northwestpartners/jobs/ (~30 open at a time as of 2026-08).
- For liveness checks on a Northwest Partners role: rely on the LinkedIn job page banner alone; there is no employer board to corroborate against.
