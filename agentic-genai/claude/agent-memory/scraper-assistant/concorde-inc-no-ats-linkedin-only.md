Concorde, Inc — LinkedIn company `linkedin.com/company/concorde-inc` (Business Consulting &
Services, Philadelphia PA, "51-200 employees", ~85 on LinkedIn). Employment Screening / Drug &
Alcohol Testing / Background Screening / Occupational Health + DOT fleet-compliance, since 1983.
Has an in-house IT dev team; remote-centric; C#/.NET shop.

- **Official website: www.concorde2000.com** (verified on their LinkedIn About tab).
  `concordeinc.com` resolves to a bare "Launching Soon" placeholder page — likely a rebrand
  domain not yet live. The LinkedIn JD's "About the Company" text cites concordeinc.com but the
  real site is concorde2000.com.
- **No ATS board of any kind.** concorde2000.com/careers and
  /about-us/employment-opportunities/ are contact-form / "email your resume to HR" landing
  pages. Page source has zero Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/Workday/Paylocity/iCIMS/etc. embeds or
  iframes. Not on Indeed cmp either ("no open jobs").
- **All requisitions live only on LinkedIn as Easy Apply** (applications managed on LinkedIn).
  ~30 open reqs, "Actively reviewing applicants", posted under Concorde, Inc directly (not a
  staffing agency). Seen Aug 2026: Software Engineer (C#/.NET, $120-135K, remote US),
  Business Analyst/Product Owner, Accounts Payable Clerk, Business Process & Research Analyst,
  + ~26 more across 3 pages.

For liveness of a Concorde role: check the LinkedIn `/jobs/view/<id>/` page directly
(authenticated) for the "No longer accepting applications" banner vs an active Easy Apply
button — there is no employer-side ATS to corroborate against.
