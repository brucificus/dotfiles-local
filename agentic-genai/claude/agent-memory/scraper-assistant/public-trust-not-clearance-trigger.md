  type: feedback

When a job-hunt kanban routing rubric lists "security clearance required (Secret, TS/SCI, Polygraph)" as a hard disqualifier trigger, do not extend that automatically to "Public Trust" clearance requirements. Public Trust is a federal suitability/background-investigation tier, distinct from a National Security clearance (Confidential/Secret/Top Secret/SCI/Polygraph) — it's common on ordinary federal-contractor corporate roles, not just defense/intel work.

**Why:** During a 2026-07-22 job-lead routing pass (28 companies), postings with "Public Trust clearance required" but an otherwise strong .NET/C# stack match (e.g. Northramp LLC) were initially at risk of being disqualified alongside genuine TS/SCI/Secret-clearance postings. Treating Public Trust as a hard disqualifier would have wrongly filtered out viable leads — the operator's rubric explicitly named only Secret/TS-SCI/Polygraph as the trigger tier.

**How to apply:** When a listing says "Public Trust clearance required" and nothing else disqualifies, route toward the fit the rest of the listing supports (e.g. "To Apply" if stack matches) rather than auto-disqualifying — but note the Public Trust requirement in the log entry as a caution. Reserve the hard disqualify for explicit Secret/TS-SCI/Top Secret/Polygraph language, or classified-information-access requirements phrased as a hard must. See [[jobhunt-routing-rubric]].
