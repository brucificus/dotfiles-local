Yes Energy (yesenergy.com, electric-power market data & trading analytics, HQ Boulder CO) careers runs on **Greenhouse**.

- Careers page yesenergy.com/careers just links out to `https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/yesenergy`
- Board slug: `yesenergy`
- List API: `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/yesenergy/jobs?content=true` (returns only live/published jobs)
- Detail API: `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/yesenergy/jobs/<id>`
- Salary range is embedded in the `content` HTML as "Salary range: $X - $Y" (Colorado law), not in structured pay fields.
- Job IDs are 10-digit (e.g. Senior Fullstack Engineer = 5362614008, Fullstack Engineer = 5362627008).

Stack: primary platform is **C#/.NET + SQL Server** (Boulder US roles). Bucharest Romania roles skew Java/Python/AI-infra and pay in RON. US engineering roles are all Boulder hybrid (2 days/office); no Remote-US engineering IC roles as of 2026-08. "Remote" postings on the board are analyst/implementation/settlements, not engineering.
