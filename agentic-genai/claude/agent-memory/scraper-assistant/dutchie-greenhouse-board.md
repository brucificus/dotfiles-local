Dutchie's official ATS is Greenhouse, board slug **`thedutchie`** (not `dutchie` — that 404s, and job-boards.greenhouse.io/dutchie says "no longer active").

- Careers page: https://dutchie.com/business/careers (the plain dutchie.com/careers 404s; footer "Careers" link points to /business/careers)
- Board: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/thedutchie
- API list: https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/thedutchie/jobs (add `/<id>` for detail with `content` HTML-escaped JD). No CSP issues, works via plain goto in attached session.

Board is small (~11 total reqs as of 2026-08). Engineering roles are sparse — often just a single "Staff Engineer | Remote". Dutchie posts to LinkedIn as promoted/off-LinkedIn-managed listings and reposts under fresh IDs; LinkedIn copies can show "No longer accepting applications" while no matching Greenhouse req exists at all (e.g. "Senior Software Engineer II" had LinkedIn 4443431247 but never appeared on the Greenhouse board).
