Strike (the Bitcoin company; legal entities Zap Solutions, Inc. / Zap Solutions Europe) hosts careers on Greenhouse.

- Board slug: `strike` — job-boards.greenhouse.io/strike
- Liveness API: `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/strike/jobs?content=true` (list, live jobs only) and `/v1/boards/strike/jobs/<id>` (detail, 200 = still open). Works via plain `goto`, no CSP/auth issues. Same pattern as [[smarterdx-greenhouse-not-ashby]].
- "Software Engineer, Backend" (5+ yrs C#, .NET Core, EF Core; Remote, US & EU time zones; US pay $177k-$199k) = GH job id 4003945004. First published 2021-03-05, an evergreen/long-running req that stays listed and gets periodically re-touched.
- The Indeed mirror posts under employer name "Zap Solutions Europe" (jk=8b1a1206782535fb).
- Strike has NO usable LinkedIn company page (linkedin.com/company/strike-me -> unavailable) and no LinkedIn job postings surface for "Zap Solutions" / this role. Other companies (e.g. Bitbo) scrape Strike's JD verbatim into their own LinkedIn posts — don't mistake those for Strike's.
